Graph basics

A graph G = (V, E) is a set of vertices joined by edges. Types: directed or undirected, weighted or unweighted, cyclic or acyclic, connected or disconnected. Representations: the adjacency list (right, under the step log) stores each vertex’s neighbours in O(V+E) space. The adjacency matrix is a V×V table in O(V²) space that answers “is there an edge?” in O(1). Toggle Directed or Weighted above and watch both change.
